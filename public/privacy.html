Privacy Policy & Personal Data Governance

Last Updated: October 2026 · Applicable to MB EM (Employee Client) & MB Admin (Manager Client)

1. Overview and Scope

Making Brands Enterprise Suite ("MB EM", "MB Admin", "we", "our") provides internal enterprise workforce management, attendance tracking, task delegation, CRM sales lead pipelines, and internal team communications for verified organization employees and administrators.

We are committed to user privacy, data minimization, and strict adherence to the Google Play Developer Program Policies. This privacy policy transparently details the data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights to delete your account and personal records.

2. Data Categories Collected & Purpose of Use

User Profile & Account Identifiers: Name, work email address, telephone number, corporate designation, and department. Used solely for authenticated access, role authorization, and internal employee directory features.
Foreground Attendance Location (Geofence): Coarse and fine GPS coordinates collected strictly at the moment the employee triggers clock-in to verify that punch-in occurs within designated workplace boundaries (250m perimeter). We NEVER collect continuous or background location.
Camera & Attendance Selfie: A single verification photo captured on-demand during clock-in. No video surveillance, continuous recording, or background camera access occurs.
Microphone (Team Voice Notes): Audio recorded strictly on-demand when the user presses and holds the voice note recording button in Team Chat. No background audio listening or call recording is performed.
Business Operational Records: Tasks, CRM leads, invoices, quotations, and expense claim receipts uploaded via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker.

3. Prohibited & Excluded Data Practices

To uphold maximum privacy and comply with Google Play permissions policies:

  • No Call Log Access: The app does not request READ_CALL_LOG or WRITE_CALL_LOG. Call records are never inspected.
  • No Telephony Interception: PROCESS_OUTGOING_CALLS and READ_PHONE_STATE are removed. Outgoing client calls are launched via the user's default phone dialer (Intent.ACTION_DIAL).
  • No Device Contact Book Harvest: READ_CONTACTS is excluded. The app operates exclusively on internal CRM leads.
  • No Spyware or Covert Call Recording: Auto-recording of phone calls is strictly disabled and prohibited.

4. Data Security, Backup, & Encryption

All communication between MB EM, MB Admin, and our Firebase cloud infrastructure is encrypted using TLS 1.3 over HTTPS. Offline databases on Android devices are sandboxed in private app directories. Furthermore, Android 12+ Data Extraction Rules explicitly exclude authentication tokens and offline database caches from Google Drive cloud backups to eliminate credential leakage.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Requirement, users have the right to delete their account and associated personal data at any time:

1

In-App Deletion: Open MB EM or MB Admin → Settings / Profile → Tap "Privacy Policy & Data Safety" → Tap "Delete Account".

2

Web Request Portal: If you have uninstalled the app or do not have access to your device, submit an account deletion request online at makingbrands.com/delete-account.

Upon confirmation, all personal identity tokens, biometric associations, and profile fields are permanently wiped from production databases within 30 days.
