Request Account & Data Deletion

Use this portal if you cannot access the MB EM or MB Admin Android app and wish to permanently delete your account and personal data.

What happens when you submit this request:

  • Your employee account profile, login credentials, and phone number association will be permanently erased.
  • Your stored face/selfie attendance snapshots and personal biometric data references will be removed.
  • Personal records are deleted from active databases within 30 days of verification.
